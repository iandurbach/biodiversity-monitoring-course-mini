The lizard icon is “Black lizard silhouette”, published by
publicdomainvectors.org on FreeSVG, under CC0 / public domain:
https://freesvg.org/black-lizard-silhouette

Downloaded image: https://freesvg.org/img/1574159691black-lizard--freesvg.org.png

Rebuild the standalone A3 PDF with:
`Rscript wildlife-survey-games/scripts/build_plot_landscape.R`

The sheet contains 300 monitors, generated with seed 64201. Columns F–J
are green (50 cells, 120 monitors); A–E are open (50 cells, 180 monitors).
Green density is exactly two-thirds of open density. Animals are allocated
randomly among cells within each habitat. Icon positions use seed 64202,
with random placement and two facing directions, clear of grid lines and
other symbols. Counts and positions are saved under data/instructor/.
All map and task counts originate in scripts/simulate.R.
